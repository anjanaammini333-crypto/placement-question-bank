How to practise

1. Choose an interview type and target role.

2. Read each question carefully.

3. Type your answer as if you were speaking to an interviewer.

4. Use the reflection checklist after each answer.

5. Review your report and practise again.

This prototype provides practice prompts and self-reflection scoring. It does not yet use AI to judge answers, listen to speech or automatically analyse your performance.

Your responses remain in this browser session for this practice. Avoid entering sensitive personal information.
